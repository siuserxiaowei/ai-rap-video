## FAQs About the AI Rap Video Generator

### 1. What is an AI rap video generator?

An AI rap video generator helps create rap performance clips or music video visuals from inputs such as images, prompts, reference videos, or audio. Tools differ: some generate original lyrics, beats, and vocals, while others animate characters or create visuals for an existing track. In Media.io, choose a workflow that supports the inputs and output you need.

### 2. Can I create an AI rap video from my own image?

Yes, use an image-compatible video mode and a clear image of yourself or a consenting adult. Show the face, hairstyle, and intended outfit clearly. A solo performance can start from one image; a rap duo may require separate character references or one combined image. Available inputs depend on the selected model.

### 3. Can I turn rap lyrics or a song into a music video?

Lyrics can guide a scene concept, while a supported audio-to-video workflow can use an existing track to guide music video visuals. Writing lyrics in a visual prompt does not by itself guarantee sung or rapped vocals. Check whether your selected tool accepts audio, generates music, or needs a separate editing step to combine the track and video.

### 4. Does the AI rap video include sound and accurate lip-sync?

Sound and mouth synchronization depend on the selected model and mode. Some workflows support native audio or supplied audio; others generate visual clips only. For fast rap delivery or two performers, review each vocal part carefully. Precise lip-sync needs compatible audio conditioning or a dedicated synchronization tool.

### 5. Is the AI rap video generator free to use?

Check your account’s current credits and the selected model’s generation cost before creating a video. Trial access, paid credits, resolution, duration, and watermark options can vary. Do not assume that every rap video mode or export setting is free; review the options shown in the tool.
