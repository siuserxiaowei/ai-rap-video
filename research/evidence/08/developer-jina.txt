## Questions & Answers

### What is Media.io API?
![Image 29: down](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/down-icon.svg)![Image 30: up](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/up-icon.svg)

Media.io API is a developer platform that allows you to integrate AI-powered video and image generation capabilities directly into your applications, products, or services.

### How is the Media.io API different from other AI providers?
![Image 31: down](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/down-icon.svg)![Image 32: up](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/up-icon.svg)

The Media.io API distinguishes itself as a comprehensive, all-in-one media processing solution. Unlike providers that specialize in a single modality (only text or only image), Media.io offers a unified interface for video, image, and audio generation and editing. Furthermore, we aggregate and integrate the world's leading AI models (such as Veo, Kling, and others) into a single platform. This allows developers to access best-in-class capabilities for every specific task-whether it's video enhancement, vocal removal, or generative creation-without needing to manage multiple vendor subscriptions or complex integrations.

### What models does Media.io support?
![Image 33: down](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/down-icon.svg)![Image 34: up](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/up-icon.svg)

Media.io leverages a powerful ensemble of proprietary and top-tier third-party foundation models to ensure optimal results for every use case. Our platform supports integrations with cutting-edge models including:

**Video Generation:** Veo 3.1, Sora 2 (via integration), Kling, Hailuo 2.3, Wan 2.2, Vidu Q3, Pixverse, and Runway.   
**Image & Processing:** Nano Banana (proprietary), Nano Pro, and specialized algorithms for upscaling, restoration, and style transfer.

This multi-model approach ensures you always have access to the state-of-the-art technology best suited for your specific creative requirements.

### Can I use Media.io for commercial projects?
![Image 35: down](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/down-icon.svg)![Image 36: up](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/up-icon.svg)

Yes, absolutely. The Media.io API is designed for professional and commercial integration. By subscribing to our API plans, developers and businesses are granted the rights to use the generated assets within their commercial applications, products, and services. We offer flexible licensing tiers to accommodate everything from startups to large-scale commercial deployments.

### Is my data private and secure?
![Image 37: down](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/down-icon.svg)![Image 38: up](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/up-icon.svg)

Data privacy and security are paramount at Media.io. We employ enterprise-grade encryption for data transmission and storage. Our platform is built with strict compliance measures, including Input & Output Sensitivity Checks, to ensure content safety. We do not use your private API data to train our foundational models without your explicit consent, and we provide features such as Voice Clone Deletion to give you full control over your assets.

### Does Media.io support enterprise workloads?
![Image 39: down](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/down-icon.svg)![Image 40: up](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/up-icon.svg)

Yes, the Media.io API is built to scale. Our infrastructure is designed to handle high-concurrency enterprise workloads, ensuring reliable performance and low latency even during peak usage. We offer dedicated support, custom rate limits, and tailored solutions for enterprise partners who require robust, high-volume processing capabilities for their applications.

### Does the Media.io API have a Playground for testing?
![Image 41: down](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/down-icon.svg)![Image 42: up](https://images.media.io/images2025/home/up-icon.svg)

Yes. We understand the importance of testing before integration. Developers can evaluate the capabilities of our models and features directly through our web-based platform, which serves as a visual playground. This allows you to verify the quality and output of specific models (like Vidu, Kling, or our enhancement tools) using your own inputs before committing to code implementation. Comprehensive documentation is also provided to streamline your testing and development process.
