## Questions & Answers

Is it safe for my uploaded files?

Absolutely. We understand the concerns about your privacy. These are some steps that we will follow to protect your files.

1. For user data security, all uploaded files and generated content on Media.io are automatically deleted from our servers within 7 days. This ensures that your data remains private and protected.

2. There is no backup made of user files.

3. You keep the copyright and ownership of the source and converted files.

Can I really cancel anytime?

Yes. Your monthly or annual subscription automatically renews, and you will have free updates in upcoming versions until you [cancel it](https://accounts.wondershare.com/web/subscription) at any time.

In what ways can I pay for my purchase? What should I do if I have a payment issue?

The following payment methods are available to our customers: JCB, Paypal, AliPay, Ukash, Diners Club, Qiwi Wallet, Discover/Novus, American Express, Chinese Debit Card, Bank/Wire Transfer, VISA/MasterCard/Eurocard.

Please note: When you pay with e-checks, there will usually be a 3 to 5-day hold on the check so that it can clear the bank. Once it has cleared, PayPal sends an automatic notification to us, and only then will the delivery of the software be completed.

If you have trouble paying, please feel free to contact us.

What should I do after I've paid?

Within 10 minutes of purchase, you will receive an email with a registration code in the email account you used to make the purchase. Sign in to your account and activate it. After that, you could enjoy your features.

What does the above "start from" price refer to?

The monthly unit prices listed on this page are determined by the annual subscription prices of the products. We offer various subscription plans to cater to your needs. Please note that the prices shown on this page are for reference purposes only. To view the complete subscription plan for each product, please click the 'Buy Now' button and visit the product's purchase page for more details.
