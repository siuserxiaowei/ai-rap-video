## Frequently Asked Questions

### How accurate is the lip sync feature?

+−

DomoAI utilizes advanced machine learning algorithms to ensure high accuracy in matching mouth movements with the audio, providing a realistic animation experience.

### Is DomoAI's lip sync generator free to use?

+−

Yes! You get free credits to start, allowing you to test the technology before committing to a paid plan.

### Can I use my own voice or another language?

+−

Absolutely! You can upload any audio file in any language, record directly in the app, or use our extensive text-to-speech options.

### Can I create a talking anime character?

+−

Yes! This is one of DomoAI's specialties. Our AI is specially trained for anime and stylized characters, making us the go-to choice for anime creators.

### Do I need technical skills?

+−

Not at all! Our advanced AI lip-sync technology automates the heavy lifting, with no editing, filming, or animation skills required.

### How fast is the processing?

+−

Most videos are generated in under 5 minutes, making it one of the fastest solutions available for creating talking avatars.
