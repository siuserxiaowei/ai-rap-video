## Frequently Asked Questions

What is OpenArt AI?

OpenArt is an all-in-one platform for creating AI images and videos. Whether you want a product ad, a TikTok reel, a short film, or a virtual character that posts content for you, you can make it all on OpenArt, without specialized editing software, design background, or technical skills.

Can I use what I create on OpenArt for my business or brand?

Yes. You fully own what you create and can use it however you like, in ads, on social media, in client work, on merchandise, or anywhere else. No royalties, no attribution required.

Can I create the same character across different scenes and videos?

Yes! This is one of OpenArt's most-loved features. Create a character once, and you can place them in any outfit, scene, or video with the same face and look every time. It's how creators build AI influencers, recurring brand mascots, and full stories with consistent characters.

Will my videos fit TikTok, Reels, and YouTube?

Yes. OpenArt exports in every format and aspect ratio you need: vertical for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts; widescreen for YouTube and ads; square for Instagram feeds; and cinematic ratios for film. Just pick the platform and your video comes out ready to post.

Do I need to know how to use AI or edit videos to get good results?

Not at all. OpenArt is built for everyday creators. You describe what you want in plain words, pick a style, and the platform handles the rest. If you want more control, advanced tools are there when you're ready and you can always refine your prompts, but you can still make great content from your very first try.

Is OpenArt free?

Yes. You can start creating on OpenArt for free with a trial plan, with daily credits and no credit card required. Paid plans unlock the most powerful AI models, longer video generation, and higher quality exports.

Which AI models does OpenArt use?

OpenArt brings together the best AI models in one place, including Seedream 5.0, Seedance 2.0, Google Veo 3, GPT Image 2, Nano Banana Pro, Sora 2, Kling 3.0, Grok Imagine, MiniMax Hailuo, and HappyHorse. Instead of paying for several separate tools, you get them all under one subscription and can pick whichever one fits the content you're making.

Can OpenArt make videos with talking, music, and sound?

Yes. You can make videos where characters speak with synced lip movements, add original AI-generated music, and include realistic sound effects, all inside OpenArt. Great for everything from quick social clips to full cinematic videos.

How is OpenArt different from tools like Midjourney, Runway, or Sora?

Most AI tools specialize in one thing. Midjourney is mainly for images, Sora and Runway are built for video. But OpenArt brings images, videos, characters, music, and editing into one place, with every leading AI model built in. You don't need five different subscriptions or constantly move files between separate apps.

Is my work private and safe on OpenArt?

Yes. Your creations stay private, payments are handled securely, and OpenArt has safeguards in place to keep the platform safe and responsible, so you can create with confidence whether you're creating for personal or professional use.
