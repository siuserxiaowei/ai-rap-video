## Agent ready by default

Every endpoint ships with an MCP server, llms.txt, and typed schemas — so Claude, Cursor, and your own agents can generate video without glue code.

[MCP](https://developers.heygen.com/mcp/overview)[CLI](https://developers.heygen.com/cli)Copy llms.txt for your agent

⏺ Using heygen:generate_video…

⏺ render complete ✓ (42s)

→ welcome_v1.mp4 · ready to ship
