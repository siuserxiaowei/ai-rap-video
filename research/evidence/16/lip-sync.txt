## FAQs

### What is lip syncing?

AI lip sync is the process of synchronising speech with a character’s lip movements in a video. You can typically create dialogue via text-to-speech inputs or by uploading an audio file that contains what you want the character to say.

### Why use our AI lip sync tool?

Our AI lip sync tool lets you create incredibly realistic lip movements that ensure the audio rhythm and tone are reflected on the character’s face accurately. On top of that, the final audio of the lip sync video is always high-quality, so any custom-written dialogue will come across very clearly.

### Is our lip sync AI free?

Yes. You can use our tool to create a realistic lip sync video without having to pay any costs or even share any credit information via free trial. For this reason, even if your budget is limited, you can freely test our tool and use it to produce unique lip sync videos that will astound viewers.

### How long does it take to lip sync a video?

Not long at all. You can typically expect our lip sync video AI to complete the lip syncing process within minutes. However, keep in mind the amount of time our tool takes can sometimes vary from video to video.

### Is our lip syncing AI tool easy to use?

Yes. Our AI lip sync tool is designed to be simplistic and intuitive. So, it can be accessed by anyone including first-timers without much hassle. Moreover, our tool can be used on any device, be it mobile, tablet, or desktop. This lets you create a believable lip sync video, even if you're on the move.

### Can I customize my lip syncing video?

Absolutely. You can use the text-to-speech option to write out the dialogue or record and upload the audio that you want in the video. There are also multiple AI voice avatars and different emotional responses to choose from. This makes it easy for you to create personalized AI lip sync videos.

### Which audio formats does our lip sync video AI support?

You can upload MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC, M4A, and OGG files. With this many options, you won’t have to worry about having to convert files beforehand. However, keep in mind you can only upload up to a maximum file size of 20MB. Moreover, the audio length can’t be longer than 30 seconds max.

### What video formats does our lip sync video AI support?

You can upload videos in MP4 or MOV formats. However, our AI lip sync tool only accepts a maximum file size of 100MB. Furthermore, the original video’s resolution needs to be at least 720p. This will ensure the final lip sync video output the tool generates will also be in high-quality.

### Who can use our AI lip syncing tool?

Anyone looking to generate a lip sync animation with lifelike speech can benefit from our tool. This includes content creators, filmmakers, artists, and even hobbyists. So, whether it is for personal or professional purposes, our lip syncing tool can prove valuable to any video creator.

### Will my audio files remain secure and private?

Definitely. Our lip syncing tool employs enterprise-grade encryption measures that protect your audio files from any unauthorized third-parties. We also won’t store any of your audio files in our database, either. Plus, our privacy policy guarantees any user data will remain confidential!
