## [​](https://docs.pollo.ai/#overview)

Overview

Pollo AI’s all-in-one API platform revolutionizes how developers and businesses harness the power of cutting-edge AI image and video generation.With full access to the world’s top AI models, including Kling AI, Runway, Hailuo AI, Vidu AI, Luma AI, Pika AI, PixVerse AI, Wanx AI, Seaweed, Veo 2, and Hunyuan, Pollo AI empowers you to integrate advanced AI capabilities into your applications seamlessly.Our platform delivers high-quality, scalable solutions at a fraction of the cost of Fal.ai, making AI innovation accessible and affordable.
## [​](https://docs.pollo.ai/#why-pollo-ai-api-stands-out)

Why Pollo AI API Stands Out

### [​](https://docs.pollo.ai/#comprehensive-ai-model-support)

Comprehensive AI Model Support

Pollo AI offers access to a wide range of advanced and comprehensive AI models across multiple platforms. For instance, you can leverage Kling AI with its versions like 1.0, 1.5, 1.6, and 2.0, as well as models from Runway, Hailuo AI, Vidu AI, Luma AI, Pika AI, PixVerse AI, Wanx AI, Seaweed, Veo 2, and Hunyuan.
### [​](https://docs.pollo.ai/#versatile-functionality)

Versatile Functionality

Our APIs support a wide array of tasks, including text and image-to-video generation, video upscaling and extending, and more. This versatility allows you to tackle various creative challenges with ease.
### [​](https://docs.pollo.ai/#budget-friendly-excellence)

Budget-Friendly Excellence

Enjoy premium AI tools at competitive prices that outperform alternatives like **Fal.ai**. This ensures you can maintain high-quality standards while keeping your projects cost-effective.
