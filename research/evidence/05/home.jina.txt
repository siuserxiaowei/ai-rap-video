## What is an AI rap video?

An AI rap video is a short music video in which real people from your photos rap an original song. RapVideo AI writes the lyrics, makes the beat, generates the vocals and renders the performance, lip-synced, from one or two photos. You don't film, record or edit anything.

It's also called the Hotel Lobby AI, Migos AI or rap duo trend: two people trading verses in a bright orange booth under a hanging mic, inspired by a famous live rap performance. With RapVideo AI you can make that duo version, a solo AI rapping video of just you, or a rap battle between two friends.

Every AI rap video is rendered by a state-of-the-art video model with native audio, in vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, or square and widescreen if you prefer.

FAQ
## AI rap video FAQ

Can't find your answer? Email us at [support@rapvideoai.com](mailto:support@rapvideoai.com) and we'll get back to you.

### Is the AI rap video generator free?

No. Videos are paid with credits: a standard song (Standard model, 720p, 12 seconds) is 10 credits, from $9.99 for one song or about $2.99 a song on the Basic plan. A render that fails is never charged.

### Can I use just one photo?

Yes. Choose Solo to make an AI rapping video of one person, or "One photo of both" if you have a single photo with the two of you in it.

### Can I use my own song?

No. RapVideo AI writes an original rap and beat around your topic. You can't upload your own song or recording.

### How do I do the Hotel Lobby trend?

Add two photos, keep the default Hotel Lobby (orange) stage and tap Make our rap video. See the [Hotel Lobby AI guide](https://rapvideoai.com/hotel-lobby-ai) for photo tips and topic ideas.

### Is it the real Hotel Lobby song?

It depends on the stage you pick. Original remake uses the real Hotel Lobby recording and choreography, with your faces on the two rappers. Any other stage makes a New song: an original AI rap and beat in a similar energetic style. We don't clone anyone's voice. RapVideo AI is independent, not affiliated with Migos, Quavo, Takeoff, Offset, COLORS or the song's rights holders.

### How long is the video, and how long does it take?

The default is 12 seconds, and you can choose 5 to 15 seconds (up to 30 with Ultra). Longer videos cost more credits. Most videos are ready in 2–3 minutes.

### Can I post it on TikTok or Instagram?

Yes. Paid videos download as HD MP4s without a watermark. New song videos use original AI music, so the audio won't match a copyrighted track. Original remake videos use the real Hotel Lobby recording, so platforms may mute, limit or remove them, and you're responsible for how you share them.

### What photos work best?

One clear, front-facing face per photo, well lit, without sunglasses, masks or heavy filters. Half-body or full-body shots look best on stage. The photos don't need to be taken together.

### Can we choose what we rap about?

Yes. Type names and one detail, like a birthday, an inside joke or your coffee shop, or tap a quick idea under the text box. Leave it empty and you get a general hype song.

### What language is the rap in?

The rap follows the language you write the topic in. With no topic, it raps in the language of the site you are using.

### Do credits expire? Can I cancel?

Pack credits never expire. Plan credits arrive every month and expire at the end of that month: monthly plans each billing month, yearly plans in 12 monthly slices (a twelfth of the yearly credits each month). Cancel anytime from Billing. A failed render always returns its credits automatically.

### Can I gift rap videos to a friend?

Yes. Any plan or pack you bought can be turned into a [gift link](https://rapvideoai.com/gift): pick how many videos each person gets and how many people, then send the link. Each friend signs in, claims an equal share and makes their own video. You can stop the link any time and get back what nobody claimed.

### Is my photo private?

Yes. Photos are used only to render your videos, never published, shared or sold. Only use photos of people who agreed to be in the video.

### What if a video fails?

If a render fails, times out or is blocked by a safety filter, the credits go straight back to your balance. You never pay for a video you didn't get.
