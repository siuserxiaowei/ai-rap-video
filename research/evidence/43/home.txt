## What is the Hotel Lobby AI trend?

The Hotel Lobby trend comes from Quavo and Takeoff’s “Hotel Lobby” performance for A COLORS SHOW: two rappers, a hanging microphone and a monochromatic stage. In the AI version, two new faces take their places — you and a friend, a couple, or anyone you tag.

People call it the Hotel Lobby AI video, the Migos AI trend or the AI rap duo. Upload two photos and choose your performance: follow the reference clip with its original soundtrack, or create a silent freestyle video.

Choose portrait for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, or landscape for a wider view. Videos run from 5 to 30 seconds, at up to 1080P.
