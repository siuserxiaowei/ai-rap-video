## How to make a Hotel Lobby video

Making a Hotel Lobby video used to mean cutting two people out of their photos, dropping them into an orange studio and re-timing every gesture by hand. Hotel Lobby AI does it for you: here is the short version, from the first photo to the moment you post.

1.   1### Pick two clear photos

Download the MP4, add the trending Hotel Lobby sound inside TikTok or Reels if you want it, write your caption and post. 

### Tips for better results

*   Use bright, even light on both photos; shadows across the face make the likeness worse.
*   Take both photos at a similar distance so the two performers feel like they share one room.
*   Keep the two people clearly different in hair or clothing so the model never confuses who is who.
*   Hotel Lobby AI works best with one clear face per photo, so crop out other people first.
*   Do not upload celebrities, public figures or anyone whose likeness you have no permission to use.
*   If a result is not quite right, try a different pair of photos rather than the same two again.
