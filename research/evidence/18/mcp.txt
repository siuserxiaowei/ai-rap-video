What is the Music Video Generator MCP?
The Music Video MCP connects freebeat's music video generator directly to AI assistants like Claude and Cursor. Once connected, you can ask your assistant to turn a song into a finished, beat-synced music video without leaving the conversation.

Which AI agents does it work with?
Claude Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code, and other agents that support the same connection standard — all using the same freebeat setup.

What do I need to start?
A free freebeat account and a key from your profile. Add the freebeat server to your agent, paste the key, and you're ready.

What can I make?
Full-length, beat-synced music videos from any song — vertical or widescreen, with your own image or character carried through every scene.

Can it use a song from Suno, YouTube, or SoundCloud?
Yes. Paste the link and freebeat pulls the audio in automatically, or upload your own audio file.

How long does a music video take?
Rendering a full music video takes a little time, so freebeat works in the background — you keep chatting and pick up the finished video when it's ready.

Do I have to put my key in every message?
No. Your key lives in the connection settings once. Your messages only describe the creative idea.
