### Seedance 2.5 draft mode

[Section titled “Seedance 2.5 draft mode”](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/models/#seedance-25-draft-mode)

Seedance 2.5 (`seedance2_5`) can render a faster 480p preview, then enhance that preview to 1080p. Use a preview to check a prompt, then pay the 1080p rate for the take you keep. See [pricing](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/pricing) for both rates.

Set `draft` to `true` on a normal [text to video](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/api#tag/Start-generating/paths/~1v1~1text_to_video/post), [image to video](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/api#tag/Start-generating/paths/~1v1~1image_to_video/post), or [video to video](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/api#tag/Start-generating/paths/~1v1~1video_to_video/post) request. Prompt, `ratio`, `duration` (including `auto`), references, `audio`, and `seed` work the same way as a full generation.

The preview is always 480p, and it is billed at the 480p rate. `ratio` still chooses the aspect ratio: a 720p or 1080p ratio is accepted, and the preview uses the 480p frame for that aspect (16:9 / 9:16 land on `854:480` / `480:854`).

`{  "model": "seedance2_5",  "promptText": "A red panda walks through a lantern-lit alley",  "ratio": "1280:720",  "duration": 5,  "draft": true}`

Poll the returned task until `status` is `SUCCEEDED`. The task id is what you pass to enhance it.

#### Enhance a preview to 1080p

[Section titled “Enhance a preview to 1080p”](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/models/#enhance-a-preview-to-1080p)

Enhancement is always a [text to video](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/api#tag/Start-generating/paths/~1v1~1text_to_video/post) request, including when the preview came from image to video or video to video. Send `model` and `draftTaskId` only. The 1080p video reuses the preview’s prompt, references, duration, seed, and audio setting — when the preview used `duration: "auto"`, the enhancement uses the measured length. An enhance request that also includes `promptText`, `ratio`, `duration`, `seed`, `audio`, references, or `draft` is rejected.

`{  "model": "seedance2_5",  "draftTaskId": "11111111-1111-4111-8111-111111111111"}`

The preview task has to belong to the same account, have succeeded, and have been created with `draft: true`. Previews can be enhanced within 7 days of creation. The result is 1080p at the preview’s aspect ratio, billed at the 1080p rate for that output duration and any input video. The preview’s charge is unchanged.
