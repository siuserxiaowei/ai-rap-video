FAQs

Questions? We have answers. Find more answers on our support site.

Contact Support

All Artist Unlimited subscription plans and credit packs are billed in USD. Credits do not expire, while subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period. You’ll receive a reminder before each renewal, and you can manage or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings.

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades begin at the start of your next billing period. Any credits you've purchased separately will always carry over.

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your plan will remain active until the end of your current billing period. Please note that all purchases are final and we don't offer refunds on subscription plans or credit packs. If you run into any issues, get in touch at support@rotorvideos.com and we'll make it right.

Credits are how you purchase individual video files on Rotor, priced in USD. To download a standalone video asset, you'll need to purchase credits first. Each download costs between one and four credits depending on the format. Credits never expire, and you can preview your video for free as many times as you like before downloading. See credit pricing above.

Premium content refers to our curated library of professionally sourced stock clips and exclusive visual styles, all included in every subscription plan. In the editor, premium clips and styles are marked with a crown icon. You can also upload your own images or footage to make every video entirely your own.

Videos created on Rotor meet Spotify and Apple Music's technical specifications and are accepted for distribution. For uploads, we accept the most common audio, video, and image formats including MP3, WAV, MP4, M4V, MOV, JPG, PNG, OGG, AAC, FLAC, and AIFF. Your final export will be delivered as an MP4, ready to distribute or share directly to social platforms.

Any video you create on Rotor is 100% yours to own, distribute, and use commercially. Exported videos are completely watermark-free. All your projects are saved in your account so you can return, edit, and re-export anytime.

Yes, you'll need to own or hold the rights to any music and artwork you upload.

Yes, credits can be used across multiple artists, so if you're managing a small roster, credits give you the flexibility to allocate videos wherever they're needed. Subscriptions, on the other hand, are tied to a single artist. If you're working across a full roster, we offer a few options for better pricing and integration at scale: Enterprise Accounts for scaled access, Plug In for white-label integration, or our Affiliate Program to earn on referrals.
