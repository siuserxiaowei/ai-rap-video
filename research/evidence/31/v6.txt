## Create from a Written Scene or Animate an Existing Visual Asset

Text-to-video is useful for new concepts. Image-to-video is useful when you already have a product image, character design, photo, or visual reference.

### Text-to-Video with PixVerse V6

Write the scene as if you were briefing a camera operator and sound designer at the same time. Identify the subject, setting, camera, motion, light, audio, and final mood.

**Prompt Example**

A matte black smart speaker sits on a walnut desk at sunrise. The camera slowly pushes in from the left. Warm light reflects on the metal rim. A soft startup chime plays as the LED ring turns blue.

### Image-to-Video with PixVerse V6

Use a product photo, character design, concept art, fashion image, real estate shot, or brand asset as a visual anchor and create motion around it without starting from zero.

**For Best Results**

Use images with a clear subject, readable edges, strong composition, and minimal ambiguity. State whether the camera pushes in, pans, tilts, tracks, or circles.

## PixVerse V6 FAQ

Quick answers for creators comparing V6 to earlier AI video workflows.

 What is PixVerse V6? 
PixVerse V6 is a PixVerse AI video generation model built for 15-second 1080p video creation, multi-shot storytelling, text-to-video, image-to-video, native audio, camera control, and marketing-ready creative workflows.

 Does PixVerse V6 support both text-to-video and image-to-video? 
Yes. PixVerse V6 can generate videos from written prompts and can animate images into motion. Text-to-video is useful for new concepts, while image-to-video is useful when you already have a product image, character design, photo, or visual reference.

 Is PixVerse V6 suitable for commercial or marketing videos? 
Yes, PixVerse V6 is well suited to marketing videos, brand concepts, social media clips, product teasers, explainers, and campaign assets. Commercial usage rights depend on the user’s PixVerse subscription tier, so teams should confirm their plan before publishing paid or client-facing work.

 How do I maintain character consistency in PixVerse V6? 
Repeat the same identity anchors across every shot description. If the character has blonde hair, a blue silk shirt, fox ears, a red jacket, or a specific product accessory, include those exact descriptors wherever the camera cuts or the scene changes.

 Can I control camera movement in PixVerse V6? 
Yes. PixVerse V6 responds well to camera instructions such as slow push-in, handheld tracking shot, low-angle shot, aerial view, POV movement, pan, tilt, orbit, and close-up. The clearer the camera language, the easier it is for the model to follow the intended motion.

 Why do PixVerse V6 videos look inconsistent between runs? 
AI video outputs can vary when prompts are vague, identity anchors are missing, or the requested scene includes too many competing actions. Use literal descriptions, repeat important character details, reduce unnecessary style terms, and review motion strength or duration settings when refining the result.
