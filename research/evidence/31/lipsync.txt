POST

/openapi/v2/video/lip_sync/generate

Provide exactly one video source. For audio, choose either audio_media_id OR both TTS fields; do not mix the two modes. Upload video and audio separately using Upload Media. Upload acceptance does not guarantee that the media meets this operation's processing requirements. Use a speaker_id returned by the TTS list.
