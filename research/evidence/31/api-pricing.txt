## Billing Overview

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Billing unit | Credits |
| USD reference | 225 Credits = USD 1; 1 Credit ≈ USD 0.004444. |
| Creation response | `Resp.credits` is the request-level Credit value. Use it for estimates and preliminary reconciliation; the account ledger determines the final charge. |

### How to Read the Tables

Each price shows **Credits** first and the **approximate USD amount** below.

**Credits/s:** multiply the rate by the billable duration. **Credits/request:** a fixed amount per request.

**Not supported:** the listed combination is unavailable. **Not listed:** no public rate is provided; this does not confirm availability.

USD amounts use `total Credits ÷ 225`, rounded to three decimal places. Calculate the Credit total before rounding the final USD amount. Plans, volume agreements, promotions, and free allowances may change your effective cost.

### Video and Effect Templates

Template prices vary. Use the selected template's `credit_consume` value from `GET /openapi/v2/video/effects/templates/list`, matching the requested quality or specification.Approximate USD amount = `credit_consume ÷ 225`. Do not reuse a historical fixed price. Listing templates does not create a billable generation task.

## Charges and Refunds

HTTP 200 with `ErrCode=0` on creation confirms request acceptance, not completed generation or final settlement. Check the final task status and output URL. For failed tasks, confirm the refund entry and restored balance; refunds may be delayed.The account ledger is the final source of truth. For support, keep the account ID, `Ai-trace-id`, `video_id`, creation time, `Resp.credits`, final status, and before/after balances.
