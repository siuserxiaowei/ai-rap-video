Billing questions

Do I need a subscription?

+

No. Buy credits once: a pack is one payment and its credits do not expire. Plans renew every month, or yearly with the year’s credits up front. The default Hotel Lobby video and the Bulin 47 Freestyle each come with a free 2-second preview: one per Google account, 480p, watermarked and on the site only. The full video, on Wan 3.0, is paid with credits.

Can I cancel anytime, and are failed renders charged?

+

Cancel anytime in Orders; the plan runs to the end of the paid period. Failed renders are refunded automatically; unused packs and unused first plan payments can be refunded within 14 days.
