Before you upload

Whatever is in the photos ends up on stage: the face, the hair, the outfit. The photos don’t need to be taken together, but each half of the rap duo needs a clear face.

![Image 1](https://rapvideo.ai/art/tip-good.svg)

Works well

*   A single person per photo, facing the camera
*   Even light on the face, no hard shadows
*   Waist-up or full body, so the outfit shows
*   JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, at least 300 px on each side, up to 25 MB

![Image 2](https://rapvideo.ai/art/tip-bad.svg)

Skip these

*   Group shots where each face is tiny
*   Heavy filters, masks or face paint
*   Sunglasses or hats that hide most of the face
*   Photos of anyone who has not agreed to be in the rap duo video
