## Hotel Lobby AI FAQs

Can I use group photos, videos, or audio with Hotel Lobby AI?
Yes. Upload two portraits or select two people from a group photo. You can also use your own reference video, song, or voice recording. Each upload field lists supported formats and file limits.

How long can a Hotel Lobby video be, and which resolutions can I choose?
We’ll list clip lengths and resolutions for each creation mode when generation opens. For a longer video, combine clips in the editor and export one MP4.

Can I use my own audio, keep the original sound, or export a silent video?
Yes. In Hotel Lobby AI, your song or recording can make the performers sing or speak. Keep sound from the selected reference section, add background music, or export a silent video. Background music does not change mouth movements. The built-in Hotel Lobby template includes a 10-second section of the Hotel Lobby track.

How much does it cost to unlock a Hotel Lobby AI video?
Review the preview before choosing to unlock the full HD video without a watermark. A standard 720p, 12-second video costs 10 credits to unlock. Choose a one-time pack, monthly plan, or yearly plan; yearly credits arrive up front.

What happens if generation takes too long or fails?
Hotel Lobby AI will show task status and let you reuse saved settings for a retry. We’ll publish queue handling and failed-task billing rules before generation opens. You’ll see any retry cost before confirming.

How do I download my Hotel Lobby video, and how long is it saved?
Watch your preview in your Hotel Lobby AI account. Once you unlock the full video, download it as an MP4. Return later or restart an interrupted download. We’ll publish the storage period before generation opens.

Can I use Hotel Lobby AI on my phone?
Yes. The Hotel Lobby template is built in, with no app or separate template to download. Upload photos in your browser; this is not a live camera filter. Generation and final rendering run in the cloud, so you don’t need a powerful computer.

How long do you keep my uploads, and can I delete them?
Your Hotel Lobby AI project files stay in your browser while you prepare them. When you continue to sign in, a local draft keeps them for up to 24 hours; select Clear saved draft to remove it. We’ll explain upload storage, processing services, and deletion controls before generation opens.
