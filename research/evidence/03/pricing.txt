## Before you buy

### Will I be charged if generation fails?

Payment places a hold on your card; you are charged only after your video is generated successfully. If generation fails, we automatically try once more. If that also fails, or your video is not delivered within 2 hours of payment, the hold is released and you are not charged. Your bank may take up to about 7 days to remove the pending hold.

### How long does it take to get my video?

Most videos are ready about 5 minutes after payment; if we need to retry automatically, it takes a little longer. You can close the page while you wait, and your video will be in My Creations once it is ready.

### Does the video have a watermark?

Your video has no visible watermark or logo. Like many AI-generated videos, the MP4 file includes Content Credentials (C2PA), metadata that marks it as AI-generated. Some platforms, such as TikTok, read this metadata and may label your post as AI-generated.

### Can I request a refund after receiving my video?

Successfully delivered videos are generally not refundable for a change of mind or dissatisfaction with the style. If your video has an obvious defect, such as the wrong number of people, missing audio or mixed-up identities, or there is a billing error, submit a refund request to support with your order number within 14 calendar days of the charge, except where mandatory applicable law provides otherwise. Requests are reviewed individually; approval is not guaranteed. Your statutory rights are unaffected. See the refund policy for the full terms.

[Contact support](mailto:support@rapreel.com)[Refund policy](https://rapreel.com/refund-policy)

### Where can I find and download my video later?

Sign in with the account you used to buy the video and open My Creations from your account menu. You can play and download your delivered video as an MP4. Videos stay in My Creations while your account exists; access may be revoked after a refund. You can also save a copy to your device.
