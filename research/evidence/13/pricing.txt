## Pricing questions

How many Credits does one video use?
It depends on the model and resolution. See the model and quality table above for the current 15-second Credit rates.

Why is Yearly cheaper per Credit?
The annual commitment gets the lowest Credit price. Credits are granted each month, and unused Yearly Credits can roll over within that paid annual term.

Can I cancel a subscription?
Yes. Cancellation stops the next renewal. The already-paid period remains available until it ends, subject to the applicable Credit rules.

Do Pay Once Credits expire?
No. Under the current Pay Once offer, those Credits do not expire and do not create a subscription.
