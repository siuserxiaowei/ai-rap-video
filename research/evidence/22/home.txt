## Powerful video editing made simple

### Visualizers

Variety of visualizers with customizable and automatable parameters.

### 2D and 3D space

Arrange elements in 2D or build a 3D scene with models, lights, and camera controls.

### Videos and images

Add your own videos and images, then position and animate them in 2D or 3D.

### Audio reactivity

Map visual properties to audio frequencies so they react to your music.

### Keyframe animation

Animate visual properties over time with keyframes and easing.

### Lyric videos

Generate lyrics from your track and style how they appear in sync with the music.

### Shareable projects

Share a project link with collaborators so they can open it in the editor.

### Effects

Apply effects to individual elements or the whole scene, from color adjustments to bloom and VHS.

### HDR videos

Stand out on TikTok or Instagram Reels with high contrast and vibrant colors.

## Templates

Start with a template and customize it for your music.

## Audio-reactive elements

Visualizers
Show your track as a waveform, spectrum, or another audio-driven pattern.

Shaders
Add animated backgrounds and patterns, with properties you can customize and link to audio.

## Add Images and Videos

Mix 2D and 3D content to create engaging visuals.

## Visual Effects

Color correction, auto-focus, chromatic aberration, strobe, and more.

## 3D Models

### Easy Import

Drag & Drop your model onto the timeline as if it were a video or image. Upload your models or browse thousands of 3D models from Sketchfab.

### Move & Automate

Customize your 3D model by changing its position, rotation, and scale. Select embedded animations for automatic playback. Use keyframes to animate movements.

## Camera controls

Animate with keyframes
Move through your scene and set keyframes to create a camera path.

Explore with fly mode
Fly around your 3D scene with a first-person camera using the WASD keys.

## Ambient Settings

### Lights

Add light sources to your scene and change their angle, color, and intensity. Each light source casts shadows on objects in a scene.

### Environments

You can change the mood of your visual with just a few clicks by changing the environment. Every environment has its lighting, gamma, and reflections.
