## How to make a short music promo video

Combine album art, artist text, audio, and a background template into a quick vertical MP4. No editor timeline needed.

##### 1. Choose background

Select a background video from the collection of professionally designed templates. Each template is optimized for social media and can be customized to match your style.

##### 2. Add artist info

Enter your artist name and song title. This information will be beautifully displayed in your video, helping to build your brand and make your content instantly recognizable.

##### 3. Upload artwork

Add your cover art or promotional image. This will be integrated into the video design, creating a cohesive visual identity for your music.

##### 4. Add your music

Upload your audio track and choose the section you want to feature in the promo clip.

##### 5. Export

Click export and get a vertical MP4 ready to share on Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about creating short artwork and audio promo videos with this quick tool.

### What is a music video maker?

A music video maker is a quick promo tool that combines your audio, album artwork, artist and song text, and a background video into a short MP4 clip for social posts.

### What file formats are supported?

For audio, we support MP3 and WAV files. For artwork, you can upload JPEG and PNG images. The final video is exported in MP4 format, optimized for social media platforms.

### Is there a file size limit?

Yes, audio files should be under 60MB, and artwork files should be under 10MB for optimal performance.

### What video dimensions are supported?

This quick maker exports vertical video (9:16 aspect ratio), a good fit for platforms like Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

### How long can my video be?

The quick maker is designed for short promo clips. Upload your audio, select the section you want to feature, and export that clip.

### Can I customize the video design?

Yes, you can choose a background template, upload your artwork, add artist and song text, and trim the audio section used in the export.

### Which social media platforms can I share my videos on?

The vertical MP4 export is best suited for short-form channels such as Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

### Do I need to create an account to use the music video maker?

No, you can create and download music videos without creating an account. The tool runs entirely in your browser.

### Is the music video maker free to use?

Yes, the quick music video maker is free to use for creating short promo clips from your audio and artwork.
