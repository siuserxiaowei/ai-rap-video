## Credit Cost Reference Table

Seedance costs are shown as credits per 12-second clip. Longer videos scale with scheme B: ceil(seconds × 12s_credits ÷ 12). Other models list credits per use.

Standard video = Seedance 2.0 Mini · 720p · 12 seconds = 10 credits. Higher models and longer clips use more credits (see table below).

### Seedance credits per 12 seconds

Actual charge = ceil(duration_seconds × table_credits ÷ 12), minimum 1.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens to my credits if I don't use them?

Different credit types have different validity rules:

Subscription Credits:When the cycle ends, credits won't expire immediately. A grace period of up to 1 month applies; after the grace period, unused credits from the current cycle expire, and you'll only be able to use credits issued in the next billing cycle.

One-time Purchased Credits:Valid for 365 days from the issuance date and will expire automatically if unused.

### Can I change my plan anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will take effect in your next billing cycle.

If you need to modify your plan, please email [support@airapvid.com](mailto:support@airapvid.com). I'll adjust it manually as soon as I see your message.

### Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 3-day money-back guarantee for all subscription plans. See[Refund Policy](https://airapvid.com/en/refund-policy)for more information.

### What video durations are available for each plan?

Free plan does not support video. Paid plans support various durations based on the model used.

### Which AI models can I use?

Free plan includes Nano Banana only. Paid plans include all available AI Image & Video models.
