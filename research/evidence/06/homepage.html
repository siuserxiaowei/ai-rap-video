How to Make an AI Rap Video in 4 Steps

The exact workflow behind the trend's most convincing videos — simplified so you never open an editor or write a prompt from scratch.

Follow the Guide

  1. Step 1 — Upload your performers

    Pick one clear, front-facing photo per rapper — good lighting, no sunglasses. Pets count. The template turns each photo into a multi-angle character reference automatically, which is what keeps faces consistent through every head turn.

    Tip: one photo each is enough — the template builds the character sheet for you.

  2. Step 2 — Pick a template

    Choose a concept above: Classic Duo, Birthday Roast Rap, or Pet Drops a Verse. The template presets the burnt-orange stage, the hanging microphone, the locked-off camera and the full gesture sequence — no scene description needed.

    Tip: the person you want viewers to watch goes on the RIGHT — that side gets the signature gestures.

  3. Step 3 — Generate with Seedance 2.5

    The template's motion guidance keeps the timing, gestures and framing of the original performance while your performers take the stage. Set 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts (16:9 for YouTube), then hit generate — your video renders in minutes.

    Tip: 10-15 seconds matches the trend's recognisable section; longer clips work too, up to 30s.

  4. Step 4 — Review and post

    Preview your track: check the lip sync during the arm-across-face moment and fast finger moves. Happy with it? Download clean, watermark-free and post straight to your feed — the hook does the rest.

    Tip: if a face drifts, regenerate once with a sharper original photo — usually all it takes.
