FAQ

## Billing without surprises

Plan selection changes how credits are added, not the visibility of the cost before a job.

How do credits work?+
Credits are charged per AI rap video. The amount is shown before you submit. A 12-second, 720p video costs 84 credits.

Do unused credits roll over?+
Yes. Credits are added to one account balance instead of resetting each month. They remain available while your account is active.

Can I buy credits without a subscription?+
Yes. Buy a single video with a one-time payment, or choose a monthly plan. One-time purchases do not renew.

What happens if a job fails?+
Reserved credits are restored when a generation is confirmed failed. If the provider outcome is uncertain, check the original request before starting another video.

How do I change or cancel a subscription?+
Open your account to manage your subscription with its payment provider. Each payment provider has its own billing controls.
