What is an AI rap video?
An AI rap video is a short generated performance with rap vocals, a beat and animated performers. Here, your photos guide two people, and your scene and topic guide the performance.

Can I use two separate photos?
Yes. Upload a portrait of each person in two-photo mode. You can also use a single photo of both people in duo-photo mode. Do not upload the same portrait twice.

Does the AI rap video include sound?
The model is configured to generate native audio, and the prompt asks for original rap vocals and a beat. The examples include real generated sound. Voice quality, lyrics, lip sync and likeness vary.

Can I choose the rap topic?
Yes. Add a short theme such as a birthday roast or best-friend celebration. Keep it within 240 characters. Leaving it blank asks for a friendly, playful rap.

What language is the rap in?
The rap is written in the language of the page you are using: English, Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, French or Italian. If you write your topic in a different language, the rap follows the topic. Pronunciation and lyrics can vary by language.

Is the AI rap video generator free?
Each 12-second AI rap video costs 84 credits. Buy a single video or choose a monthly plan to add credits. Signing up does not include free credits.

What can I download?
A completed AI rap video is saved as an MP4 with sound. Choose 9:16 for portrait, 1:1 for square, or 16:9 for landscape. The initial configuration is 12 seconds at 720p.

Whose photos can I upload?
Only upload people who agreed to appear, and images you have the rights to use. Adult or NSFW content is prohibited. Do not use the tool to impersonate someone or create deceptive endorsements.
