## Create in 5 Steps

From Track to Masterpiece: Quick Start Guide.

01

### Start & Upload Your Track

Name your project and upload the music file you wish to create a video for.

02

### Generate Clips & Starter Frames

Use the left workspace to generate images and videos. Images can be used as the precise first frame for subsequent video clips.

03

### Refine Lip Sync Precision

Drag and drop the music track from the editor onto the dedicated lip-sync area to guide more accurate video generation for vocals.

04

### Assemble on the Timeline

Drag your generated video clips to the main video track. Arrange them in your desired sequence and customize their timing.

05

### Export and Share

Finalize your masterpiece! Export your high-definition music video and share your work with the world.
