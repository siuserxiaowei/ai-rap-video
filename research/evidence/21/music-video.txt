## How to transform your music link into an amazing music video with Revid in 3 steps

### 1 Upload your track

Choose your music file - audio or video. The AI handles both formats perfectly. Quality matters here. Better input, better output. Even if your file isn't studio-grade, the AI can still produce impressive results.

### 2 Select your style

Choose your visual preference: stock videos, AI-generated clips, or moving AI images. Each offers unique advantages. Stock is efficient, AI-generated is customized, moving images balance both. Your choice shapes the final product. Select what suits your vision best.

### 3 Generate and refine

Hit 'Generate' and let the AI work its magic. In minutes, you'll have a video. Then, use the editor to enhance. Adjust timings, swap visuals, fine-tune captions. This is where you add your personal touch. Make it uniquely yours.

01

## Make a music video from an MP3, WAV or Suno track

Upload your finished song or paste a supported music link. Revid uses that recording as the soundtrack while it builds the visuals. Use the audio trim control to start with a chorus or a short release teaser, or keep the full track selected. For a Suno song, provide the share link or upload the audio file you downloaded. Choose the output ratio for your destination before generating: vertical for a short social clip or landscape for a YouTube music video.

02

## Choose beat sync for instrumentals or lyric timing for vocals

The generator has separate Lyrics and Beats timing options. Choose Beats when you want visuals paced to an instrumental, dance track or beat. Choose Lyrics when the words should guide the scenes, then review the detected lyrics and caption timing in the editor. A dense mix or an unusual pronunciation can need corrections. Singer lip sync is a separate option for shots where an on-screen performer should sing; it is different from putting lyric captions over a scene.

03

## Plan the visuals before generating a full song

Pick a subject, a setting and a visual style that can carry several scenes. For a night-drive track, a direction like 'a lone driver, wet city streets, blue reflections, slow tracking shots' gives the visuals a consistent starting point. Try a short excerpt first so you can judge the pacing and look before spending credits on the full song. AI video, moving images and stock footage produce different results and have different credit costs; check the estimate shown before generation.

04

## Review the scenes, captions and ending before export

Open the generated video in the browser editor and watch it with the original track. Replace a scene that does not fit the lyric, adjust a cut that interrupts a phrase, and correct names or words in the captions. Keep text away from the edges for mobile viewing. Check the last shot as carefully as the first: a held final frame can suit a sustained note better than an abrupt cut. Export the finished video, or use Revid's publishing tools when you are ready to post.
