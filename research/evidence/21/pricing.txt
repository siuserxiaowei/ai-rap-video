## Pricing questions

How much does Revid AI cost?
Growth is $39/month, or $32/month billed yearly, with 2,000 AI credits a month. Ultra is $199/month, or $166/month billed yearly, with 12,000 credits. Subscribers can also move to Elite at $89/month, or $74/month billed yearly, with 5,000 credits. For higher volume, Ultra scales to 30,000 credits ($459/month), 50,000 credits ($749/month), 100,000 credits ($1,459/month) and 200,000 credits ($2,799/month).

Is there a free plan or free trial?
You can sign up without a credit card, and new accounts get free credits to try video generation. A free account can also search viral videos. Regular creation uses the monthly credits that come with a paid plan.

How many videos can I make with 2,000 credits?
It depends on the visuals. A typical 30-second video with Pro moving images and a voiceover costs about 40 to 80 credits, so 2,000 credits cover roughly 25 to 50 of them a month. Full AI video models cost more per second. The editor shows the estimated cost before every render.[See every credit cost](https://www.revid.ai/llm/revid-ai-credit-costs.md).

Do unused credits roll over?
Plan credits refill to your plan amount at each renewal; they do not stack month to month. Credit Boost packs are one-time top-ups that do not expire: 1,000 credits for $49, 4,000 credits for $99, 10,000 credits for $199 and 100,000 credits for $1,900.

What is included in every plan?
Every plan includes the 100+ AI video tools, voiceovers in 70+ languages, direct publishing to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube with post stats, Auto-Mode workers, and full access to the Revid API, MCP server and CLI. Plans differ by monthly credits and the number of Auto-Mode workers.

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel from your account settings at any time, your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you keep full ownership of the videos you made.
