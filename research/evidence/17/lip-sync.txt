## AI Lip Sync FAQ

### What is AI lip sync?

AI lip sync takes a video of a person talking and a new audio track, then re-animates the mouth so it matches the new words. GoEnhance keeps the rest of your footage as it was filmed.

### How long can my video be?

Each lip sync can be 3 to 60 seconds long. Upload MP4 or MOV files up to 50MB. Audio files can also be up to 60 seconds.

### What happens if the audio is longer than the video?

By default the result is as long as the shorter of the two. Turn on loop mode to play the video back and forth so the full audio fits.

### Which languages does lip sync support?

Any spoken language. The mouth follows the sounds in your audio, so it works for English, Spanish, Japanese, French, Portuguese and more. The examples on this page were dubbed from English.

### How much does AI lip sync cost?

With the default v2 model it costs 2 tokens per second at 540p and 3 tokens per second at 720p, so a 20-second clip at 540p costs 40 tokens.

### What is the difference between lip sync and a talking avatar?

Lip sync starts from a video and only changes the mouth. A [talking avatar](https://www.goenhance.ai/talking-avatar) starts from a single photo and animates the whole face. Use lip sync to dub or fix existing footage, and a talking avatar when you only have an image.

### What kind of video gives the best result?

A clip where one face is clearly visible, well lit and roughly facing the camera, with clean speech in the audio and little background noise. If several people are on screen, use the v1 model to pick which face to sync.
