### Frequently asked questions

What is neural frames?

neural frames is the complete AI music video platform trusted by 10,000+ artists worldwide. We offer three powerful creation modes: Autopilot (song-to-video in 2 clicks), Frame-by-Frame Editor (unprecedented control over every frame), and Text-to-Video Editor (timeline-based editing with Kling, Seedance, and Runway). With true audio-reactive capabilities, your visuals pulse and flow with your music through 8-stem analysis. We're the only platform offering character consistency, automatic lyric extraction, and professional 4K output—all designed specifically for musicians.

Do I own the rights on my videos?

Yes, you have complete ownership of all videos created with neural frames. We claim zero rights to your content and never use your videos without explicit permission. You retain full commercial rights to monetize, distribute, and use your videos however you want—on YouTube, Spotify, social media, or anywhere else. You're responsible for ensuring you have rights to any copyrighted material you include (music, reference images), but the AI-generated visuals and final videos are 100% yours.

What are standard models?

neural frames gives you access to the world's leading AI video models in one platform. Choose from Kling (exceptional natural movement and cinematic quality), Seedance (artistic and stylized transformations), Runway (industry-standard professional output), and Stable Diffusion (unique abstract aesthetics). Each model has distinct strengths—Kling excels at realistic motion, Seedance creates dreamlike sequences, Runway delivers consistent quality, and Stable Diffusion powers our trippy frame-by-frame animations. Switch between them instantly to find your perfect visual style.

What are custom models?

Custom models let you train AI on your unique visual identity. Upload 10-20 images to create consistent characters—perfect for featuring yourself, band members, or fictional avatars across multiple videos and entire albums. For custom visual styles, upload 50-100 images to train the AI on your aesthetic. This game-changing feature enables true character consistency across projects, branded content creation, and concept albums with recurring visual themes. Your custom models become part of your creative toolkit, ready to use across all your projects.

What is upscaling?

Upscaling transforms AI-generated videos into crisp, broadcast-quality output up to 4K resolution. While most AI video platforms charge premium fees for HD output, neural frames includes intelligent upscaling on every export at no extra cost. Our specialized AI enhancement ensures your videos look professional on any screen—from phone displays to cinema projectors. Whether you're creating for YouTube, Instagram, or professional presentations, your videos automatically receive this quality boost without any additional steps or fees.

How does music visualization work?

neural frames offers the most advanced audio-reactive system available. We automatically extract 8 different stems from your track (drums, bass, vocals, melody, etc.) and give you precise control over how visuals respond to each element. With 10+ modulation parameters, you can make colors pulse with the kick drum, transitions follow the melody, or effects intensify with vocals. Unlike basic visualizers, our system truly understands your music's structure, energy, and emotion—creating videos that don't just match your beat but amplify your artistic vision. Perfect for electronic music, hip-hop, rock, or any genre where the visuals should dance with the sound.

How does animation from audio work?

We are all for simplicity, and there extract key features of your song completely automatically, such as lyrics, the key, and the tempo. Those will be used completely automatically to create a video that is in sync with your music. Additionally, you can add captions to your video, creating a stunning lyric video.
