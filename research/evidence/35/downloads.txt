3.   Changes associated with the Universal Music Group ("UMG") partnership

Increased subscription credit limits, one-time 1000 credit granting, downloads disabled

February 17, 2026

**As part of this, the following changes were made to the Udio service:**

*   All subscribers have been given a one-time grant of 1000 extra, non-expiring credits.

*   The Standard subscription monthly credit limit has been increased from 1200 to 2400.

*   The Pro subscription monthly credit limit has been increased from 4800 to 6000.

*   Pro subscribers can now create 5 sets of songs (10 songs) at the same time, up from 4 sets.

**Note that downloading of audio, video, and stems has been disabled**

**[Learn more](https://www.udio.com/blog/a-new-era) about the partnership** and how it will enable Udio to offer amazing new opportunities for artists and creators.
