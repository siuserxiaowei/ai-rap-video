Title: Create a Song with Your Own Lyrics

URL Source: https://help.udio.com/en/articles/10716221-create-a-song-with-your-own-lyrics

Markdown Content:
Writing your own lyrics lets you tell stories, express emotions, and bring personal meaning to your music. Whether you want to generate lyrics quickly or craft them from scratch, here’s how to do it.

## **Generate Lyrics from a Song Description**

Need inspiration? Describe your song’s theme, and Udio will generate lyrics for you.
