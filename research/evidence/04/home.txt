## What is an AI rap video generator?

An AI rap video generator turns still photos into a performance. You give it faces; it gives you a music video in which those faces rap. Ours is built for duos: one photo for each rapper, and the model puts you side by side on a stage, writes original lyrics, produces the beat and animates both mouths so every word lands on time.

It is the quickest way to join the Hotel Lobby trend, the orange-booth duo performance that took over TikTok and Reels in September 2026, without hunting for templates or learning an editor. The two photos don't need to be taken together, so a friend on another continent works as well as the one sitting next to you.

Every clip is rendered fresh by a Seedance video model with native audio, in vertical 9:16 by default, so it is ready for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts the moment it finishes.

## AI rap video generator FAQ

### What is the AI rap video generator?

It is an online tool that turns two photos into a short music video of both people rapping together. It writes original lyrics, generates the beat and vocals, and lip-syncs everything to your faces, all in the browser with no app to install.

### How does it work?

Upload one face photo per person (or one photo of both), choose a room, optionally give the verse a topic and tap Render our rap. A language model writes a short verse for two, then a Seedance video model renders both of you performing it with native audio.

### Is it free?

No. Each render uses a paid AI video model, so videos cost credits: a standard song (720p, 12 seconds) is 10. A single song is $9.99; plans begin at $29.90 a month for 10 songs and come down to about $1 a song on the yearly Tour plan. Failed renders are never charged.

### Does it do the Hotel Lobby look?

Yes. The orange booth is the default stage, so every video starts with the look of Quavo and Takeoff's COLORS performance, with your faces and an original song. Our Hotel Lobby AI page walks through it step by step.

### What photos work best?

Use one sharp photo per person, facing the camera, in even light, with the whole face visible: skip sunglasses, masks and beauty filters. The two photos can be taken anywhere, on any day.

### Can we choose what the rap is about?

Yes. Tap "Give the verse a topic" and type an idea, for example "Sam turns 30" or "friends since first grade"; the lyrics are written around it.

### Which language do they rap in?

English. Every verse is written and performed in English. You can type your topic in any language and the lyrics will still come out in English.

### How fast is it, and how long are the clips?

Most videos finish in two to three minutes. A standard video is 12 seconds; you can pick 5 to 15 seconds, or up to 30 seconds on the Ultra model.

### Can I post the video on TikTok or Instagram?

Yes. Finished videos download in HD without a watermark, in vertical 9:16 (or square or widescreen if you choose). The song is original, not a copyrighted track.

### Am I charged if a render fails?

No. If the model errors, times out or a safety check stops the render, the credits are put back on your balance on their own.

### May I upload photos of someone else?

Only if they said yes. Minors need a guardian's consent, and the tool may not be used to pose as public figures or to mock, mislead or harass anyone.

### Do credits expire? Can I get a refund?

Pack credits stay on your account until you use them. Monthly plans add fresh credits at each renewal; yearly plans add the whole year's credits at once, valid for 12 months. An unused pack or a first plan payment can be refunded within 7 days; see the refund policy.
