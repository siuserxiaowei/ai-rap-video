## World Model Runtime

The first engine purpose built for multimodal training and inference.

Built by a frontier research lab advancing visual AI, WMR brings Hedra’s experience training and serving its own models to your workloads. Purpose-built for diffusion, video, and world models, it optimizes visual generation—from attention across frames to each denoising step.

### Vision-native kernels

Optimize attention across frames and image encoding and decoding for the demands of visual models.

### Quality-aware quantization

Reduce numerical precision with image and video quality guiding the tradeoffs.

### Step-aware scheduling

Coordinate batching and caching around generation steps to improve latency and throughput.
