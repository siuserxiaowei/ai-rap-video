## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I make an AI rap video in CapCut?

Start with a short section of your own track and decide what the viewer should see during it. Use the available AI video workflow to develop a scene from a prompt or reference image, then bring the result into your edit. CapCut's [AI video creation guide](https://www.capcut.com/resource/ai-video-generator-online) provides more workflow context. Place the music first, trim each shot to suit its rhythm, and add lyric text where it helps. Inspect the whole sequence before exporting, especially hands, facial details, and transitions. Generate one short test before spending time on a complete song.

### Can I use my own rap song and lyrics?

Yes. Import your recording into the editing project and use it as the timing reference for the video. Keep an accurate copy of your lyrics nearby, because fast delivery, layered vocals, and ad-libs can make automatic transcription less reliable. Review every caption against what you hear, then adjust its start and end points. The [lyric video workflow](https://www.capcut.com/resource/ai-lyric-video-maker) offers additional editing guidance. Use music and images you have permission to publish, and check whether their terms cover your intended release. Keep a version without captions if you also need a clean visualizer.

### Will an AI rap video automatically match every word?

Do not assume the first generated clip will follow a finished rap vocal precisely. Scene generation, body movement, and lyric timing are separate things to review. For an easier first project, use atmospheric visuals, character cutaways, or animated artwork instead of a continuous close-up performance. Align those shots and your captions to the recording in the editor. If you use a supported lip-sync feature, test a short phrase and inspect the result before extending the sequence. Clear timing and readable text can communicate the song even when the visuals do not show a mouth performing every line.

### Is the AI rap video generator free to use?

Check the current CapCut workspace for the features and credits available to your account. AI generation, specific models, premium assets, and export options may have different requirements, so this page does not promise unlimited free renders. Plan your scenes and test a short clip before committing to a longer edit. You can also compare editing approaches in the [online video editor guide](https://www.capcut.com/resource/free-ai-video-editors-online). Keep the original audio, reference images, and successful clips organized so you can revise the edit without repeating every generation. Confirm the displayed cost before each new render.
