Lipsync 2.0

## Submit a Lipsync 2.0 job

video

audio

produces

video

$0.07 per

second

Start an asynchronous job that re-renders a source video with the speaker's mouth matched to a new audio track — for dubbing, voice replacement, or a cleaner take.

**Inputs**

*   `video_url` — public URL of the source video, with one speaker. More than one speaker is not supported
*   `audio_url` — public URL of the new audio track to lip-sync the video to. To translate a video, send the translated speech

**What happens next**

The job is **accepted immediately** — you get `202 Accepted` with a `job_id` and status `PROCESSING`. Rendering usually takes a minute or two; poll `GET /v1/lipsync-2.0/{job_id}` until the job is `COMPLETED` or `FAILED`.

POST

`/v1/lipsync-2.0`

Number of seconds before the media URLs returned for this request expire. A value above the maximum is capped rather than rejected.

#### Request body[Lipsync20Input ↗](https://api.veed.io/reference/lipsync20-input)

`audio_url`url required

URL of the new audio track to lip-sync the video to.

Must be an http(s) URL.

`video_url`url required

URL of the source video to dub.

Must be an http(s) URL.
