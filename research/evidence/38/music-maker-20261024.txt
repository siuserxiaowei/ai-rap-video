## Want to know more?

What is an AI music video generator?

![Image 34: collapse](https://web-assets.invideo.io/common/prod/icons/rounded-close.svg)

An AI music video generator is a tool that creates music videos automatically using audio tracks and prompts. It combines music, visuals, and effects without needing manual editing.

How do I create a music video with AI?

![Image 35: collapse](https://web-assets.invideo.io/common/prod/icons/rounded-close.svg)

Enter a prompt, mention the type of music you want, choose the video style, and the AI will generate a music video in mere minutes.

Can I choose the style or tone of music in the video?

![Image 36: collapse](https://web-assets.invideo.io/common/prod/icons/rounded-close.svg)

Yes, you can specify the type of music, like upbeat, cinematic, or relaxing, within your prompt, and invideo AI will use it to match the video’s tone.

Do I need video editing skills to use this tool?

![Image 37: collapse](https://web-assets.invideo.io/common/prod/icons/rounded-close.svg)

No editing skills are needed. The tool handles visuals, transitions, and effects automatically, making it ideal for musicians and creators of all levels.

Where can I share the AI-generated music videos?

![Image 38: collapse](https://web-assets.invideo.io/common/prod/icons/rounded-close.svg)

You can share your music videos on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms, or use them for promotions, music releases, and fan engagement.
