### What is an AI rap video generator?

An AI rap video generator creates a complete rap music video for you. With RapVideoGenerator you upload one photo, describe what the rap should be about and pick a beat style. The AI writes the lyrics, produces an original track with a rap vocal, then animates your photo so it raps the verse in sync.

### How long does it take to make a rap video?

Lyrics are ready in a few seconds. A finished 10 or 15 second rap video usually takes 5 to 10 minutes, depending on length and queue. You can close the page; every video is saved in My Studio.

### Is the AI rap video generator free?

Sign up to receive 10 free credits—enough to make a 30-second song with two versions to choose from. The gift is once per account. All credits share one balance and can be used for music or videos. Text songs cost 10 credits for 30 seconds, 20 credits for 60 seconds or 40 credits for 120 seconds, unless covered by a paid plan’s song allowance. AI videos start at 100 credits; add a plan or a one-time credit pack from US$9.99 when you’re ready. Lyrics and rap names are free with no sign-up.

### Can I use someone else's photo?

Only with their permission. You must own the photo or have consent from the person in it. Photos of celebrities, politicians, other public figures and anyone under 18 are not allowed and are blocked by an automatic check.

### Is the music copyright free?

Every beat and vocal is generated from scratch for your video, and we never sample or imitate existing songs or named artists. Starter and credit pack videos are licensed for personal use; Pro videos include a commercial use license.

### What video format do I get?

A vertical 9:16 MP4 with the rap audio built in, sized for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Clips are 10 or 15 seconds in 480p or 720p, with 1080p on Pro and Studio. Every resolution supports custom scenes; lip-sync accuracy can vary. Prefer audio only? The AI rap generator makes full songs you can download as MP3.

### What happens if a generation fails?

If any step fails, the credits for that video are returned to your balance automatically. You can see every charge and refund in My Studio.
