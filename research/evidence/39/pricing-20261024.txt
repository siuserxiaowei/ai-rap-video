## Frequently  
Asked Questions

More questions? Reach out to our [support team](https://help.renderforest.com/support/home).

### Will my subscription plan renew automatically?

![Image 7: Expand FAQ answer](https://static.rfstat.com/renderforest/images/v2/new-homepage/arrowdown1.svg)

Any subscription plan renews automatically. If you don't want to be billed, cancel your subscription until the expiration date; otherwise, the system will bill your card automatically.

### Can I upgrade my current plan whenever I wish?

![Image 8: Expand FAQ answer](https://static.rfstat.com/renderforest/images/v2/new-homepage/arrowdown1.svg)

You can easily upgrade to a higher subscription plan by visiting the subscription page on your profile and clicking the Upgrade button in the top right corner of the [subscription](https://www.renderforest.com/subscription) box. This option helps you to upgrade by paying only the difference.

### Will I be able to export longer videos if I subscribe?

![Image 9: Expand FAQ answer](https://static.rfstat.com/renderforest/images/v2/new-homepage/arrowdown1.svg)

Yes, if you subscribe to any subscription plan, the duration limit will be extended. You can create and export longer videos in HD quality with the paid plans.

### What is migration, and why was my plan migrated to another?

![Image 10: Expand FAQ answer](https://static.rfstat.com/renderforest/images/v2/new-homepage/arrowdown1.svg)

To ensure that the newly performed updates and advantages are available for our loyal users, we have granted you an option to migrate with one click if you are already a subscriber. As we take care of our valuable users, we have removed the credit limit of your subscription plan so that you can create unlimited HD quality projects now with the same subscription plan.

### Can I pay any annual plan month by month?

![Image 11: Expand FAQ answer](https://static.rfstat.com/renderforest/images/v2/new-homepage/arrowdown1.svg)

There are both monthly and annual subscription plans available on Renderforest. All annual subscription plans are paid upfront, so if you want to pay month by month, you need to choose one of the monthly subscription plans.

### How can I upgrade my storage?

![Image 12: Expand FAQ answer](https://static.rfstat.com/renderforest/images/v2/new-homepage/arrowdown1.svg)

To upgrade your storage, you need to upgrade your subscription plan to a higher one. Please go to the [pricing page](https://www.renderforest.com/subscription) to proceed with the upgrade.

### How can I create unlimited 4K videos?

![Image 13: Expand FAQ answer](https://static.rfstat.com/renderforest/images/v2/new-homepage/arrowdown1.svg)

You need to subscribe to the Business plan to create unlimited 4K videos.

* 4k export is available on templates which do support 4K.
