# Research workbench visual decisions

The verified UI/UX Pro Max match is Data-Dense Dashboard, fitting research comparisons and evidence browsing. Use its accessible blue/gray palette and compact information structure. Its automatically suggested enterprise sales funnel and decorative motion do not fit this reader task and are not adopted.

Use the functional page contract in WORKBENCH_SPEC.md as authority for layout: sidebar/top nav, search and filters, evidence-backed catalog, three-site comparison, source details, export, and a separate creation brief flow. Chinese uses system sans-serif fallbacks, no remote font dependency. The interface must feel like a professional music research studio, without fabricated images, reviews, usage metrics or generated videos.

Use static, meaningful SVG marks or waveform accents. No parallax, auto-playing samples, required external assets or GSAP dependencies. Support reduced motion and visible keyboard focus. Contrast at least 4.5:1 for ordinary text and touch targets approximately 44px. Desktop and 390px mobile should expose the same primary actions.

The saved design-system MASTER is a recommendation snapshot, not an instruction to add Contact Sales, client logos or a marketing hero. Do not let presentation override the evidence or user task.
